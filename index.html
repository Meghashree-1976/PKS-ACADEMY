01 ABOUT PKS

Education that connects knowledge with opportunity.

PKS Foundation has grown through a commitment to education, practical learning and student development. From its beginnings in commerce and management education, the organisation has expanded into computer education and distance education.

Today, PKS Computer Education continues to focus on meaningful learning experiences that help students build knowledge, skills and confidence.
